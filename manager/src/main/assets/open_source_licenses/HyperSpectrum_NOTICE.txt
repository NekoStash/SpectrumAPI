SpectrumAPI adaptation notice
----------------------------
Reference: wxxsfxyzm/HyperSpectrum commit
cff6b634b34614531a5a9ce7cc96bf43bc7e3efa.
The preceding paragraphs are the upstream NOTICE, retained for attribution.
SpectrumAPI reuses/adapts display geometry, artwork colors, presentation motion,
media-icon and local-route semantics, and ports SystemUI/plugin callback routing,
layout compatibility, and view lifecycle. Modified files identify the adaptation.
It does not bundle HyperSpectrum Visualizer, native capture, FFT, volume recovery,
or its Zygisk Next backend. Its optional Xposed APK consumes SpectrumAPI SDK
APP_UID subscriptions instead of capturing audio itself.
SpectrumAPI projects SDK total band power by actual-Hz overlap into six display
bands and uses a fixed peak-equivalent response curve. This is a display
adaptation, not reconstruction of the original FFT and not numerical equivalence
to HyperSpectrum's native FFT aggregation. RoxyHook supplies the modern hook DSL.
Original/hyperos/ios choose color treatment only, not capture backends.
